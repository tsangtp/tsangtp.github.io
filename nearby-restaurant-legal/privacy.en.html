Privacy Policy

For the "Dinner Roulette (Nearby Restaurant Picker)" mobile application
Last updated: October 9, 2026  ·  Effective: October 5, 2026

The service is also available as a web version at https://nearby-restaurant.pages.dev, so overseas and desktop users can use it in a browser; this policy applies to the web version as well.

In one sentence: We do not create accounts, we do not collect personally identifying information, and we do not sell any data. Your favorites, history, and draw records stay on your own device. To find nearby restaurants, the app sends your precise location (latitude and longitude) to the Google Places API.

1. What We Collect

1.1 Location (while using the app only)

  • When you tap "Find nearby restaurants," the app requests when-in-use location permission (NSLocationWhenInUseUsageDescription).
  • The coordinates are sent, together with the search radius, to the Google Places API to find restaurants near you. This is the only personally related information that ever leaves your device.
  • We do not track your location in the background and do not store your movement history. You may revoke the permission at any time in iOS "Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services"; other features remain usable afterward.

1.2 Content you voluntarily enter

  • Custom wheel option names (e.g. "the noodle shop downstairs"). This text is written only to your device's local database.
  • Search keywords (e.g. "hot pot"). Keywords are sent to the Google Places API to retrieve results.

1.3 Data stored on your device

  • Favorites, search history, draw records, wheel options, language and dark-mode preferences.
  • Stored in the app's private local database (SQLite); never uploaded to our servers — we have no servers holding your data.
  • Deleting the app deletes this data. You can also clear each category in "Settings → Data" inside the app.

1.4 What we do NOT collect

  • Name, email address, phone number, or any account information (no sign-up or login).
  • Contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or health data.
  • Usage analytics or crash reporting: the App itself collects none; the advertising SDK (Google AdMob) diagnostic data is described in the "2. Third-Party Services" section.

2. Third-Party Services

2.1 Google Places API

Restaurant names, addresses, ratings, opening status, price levels, and photos are provided by the Google Places API. The app sends "precise location (latitude and longitude) / search keyword / restaurant ID" to Google to obtain them. Google's handling of those requests is governed by Google's own privacy policy:

  • https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Maps Platform Terms of Service

2.2 Our own relay server (proxy)

To keep the API key out of the app, requests first pass through a lightweight proxy we run on Cloudflare Workers. The proxy only forwards requests; it does not log or retain your location or queries, and its logs contain only non-identifying technical data (timestamp, status code).

2.3 Google AdMob (advertising)

The app uses Google AdMob to display non-personalized banner ads. AdMob may collect device information (such as device model, OS version, and coarse region) and ad-interaction data for ad delivery and invalid-traffic prevention. We do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) to track you across apps. See Google's advertising policies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

3. Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We disclose information to third parties only:

  • To deliver a feature you requested, by sending the necessary information to the Google Places API described in Section 2.
  • When legally required: we comply with valid legal process within the limits of the law.
  • Advertising: as described in section 2, the Google AdMob SDK collects device and ad-interaction data under its own policies. We do not provide your favorites, search history, or draw history to advertisers.

4. Retention and Deletion

  • Your restaurant lists and preferences exist only on your device until you delete them or remove the app.
  • Per Google Places terms, Google-sourced restaurant content (name, address, rating, etc.) is fetched on demand and not stored long-term; only the place_id is retained so the latest details can be re-fetched.

5. Children's Privacy

This app is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app collects no named personal data, we do not hold children's data in practice either.

6. Your Rights

  • Access and deletion: your data is in your hands — clear it in "Settings → Data" or delete the app.
  • Withdraw consent: disable location permission in system settings at any time.
  • If your jurisdiction (e.g. GDPR in the EU, CCPA in California) grants you additional rights, most requests can be fulfilled through the actions above since we hold none of your personal data.

7. Security

All communication with Google and the proxy server uses HTTPS. The API key is kept in a server-side secret manager and is never distributed with the app. Please understand, however, that no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the "Last updated" date on this page and, where appropriate, notify you prominently within the app. Continued use constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact Us

10. Merchant Advertising Platform (Web, Optional)

The sections above describe the general user experience of the restaurant picker. The web version also offers a separate, optional merchant advertising platform for restaurants that buy a home-screen banner. That service does involve server-side data, as follows (technical facts verifiable from the service code only):

  • Merchant account: registration requires an email address and a password. Passwords are stored as a salted PBKDF2-SHA256 hash (100,000 iterations, random 16-byte salt); the plaintext password is never stored. Sign-in issues a JWT.
  • Ad content: the banner image_url and link_url you provide are stored in a Cloudflare D1 database, together with the ad status and its start/end timestamps.
  • Payment: checkout takes place on Stripe's hosted Checkout page. Card details are entered with Stripe and are never sent to or stored by our service; we receive only the Checkout session id and a signature-verified payment webhook.
  • Duration: a paid banner is active for 7 days (BANNER_DURATION_DAYS = 7).
  • Public endpoint: /banners/active selects only the image_url and link_url of active banners from the database. The id and status fields in the response are fixed literal strings, so no internal database identifiers are exposed.

This section describes the current technical behaviour of the service only.
