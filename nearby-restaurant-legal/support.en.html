Dinner Roulette (Nearby Restaurant Picker) — iOS app and web version

Dinner Roulette picks a nearby restaurant at random. It is available both as an iOS app and as a web version at https://nearby-restaurant.pages.dev, so desktop users and users outside Hong Kong and Taiwan can use it in a browser.

Contact us

Email · tsangtakpu@yahoo.com We usually reply within 1–2 business days. To help us help you, please tell us whether you are using the iOS app or the web version. For the iOS app, include your iPhone model and iOS version. For the web version, include your browser name and version, your operating system (for example Windows, macOS, Android or iOS), and the exact website URL you were on (including the #/… part).

Frequently asked questions

1. “Start” fails, or no restaurants are found

  • iOS app: allow location access under Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Dinner Roulette → “While Using the App”.
  • Web version: allow location access for nearby-restaurant.pages.dev in your browser's site permissions, or use the map in “Choose your area” to drop a pin anywhere in the world instead.
  • Check your internet connection — restaurant data is fetched on demand.
  • If you see a rate-limit or quota message, wait a minute (or until the next day) and try again.

2. Location is inaccurate

Location precision is decided by your device, browser and surroundings. Indoors, underground or among tall buildings, the device may only report the centre of an area. Moving outdoors or enabling Wi-Fi can help. On the web version, dropping a pin on the map is the most reliable way to choose your starting point.

3. Restaurant photos do not load

Photos are served by Google Places and need a connection. Some places simply have no photo on Google; a colored placeholder built from the first character of the name is shown instead.

4. Restaurant details are wrong

Names, addresses, phone numbers, ratings and opening status all come from Google Places, not from our own database. The fastest fix is to use “Report a problem” on Google Maps; the app picks up the correction automatically.

5. A cuisine I want is missing

The “dining preferences” list covers common cuisines only. Use the free-text search to look for anything else — search is not limited to the preset categories.

6. How do I delete my data?

  • iOS app: Settings → Data lets you clear history, favourites, wheel names and draw records individually. Uninstalling the app removes all local data.
  • Web version: use your browser's site-data controls to remove locally stored preferences and history.

For general users of the restaurant picker we do not create accounts and do not upload your favourites or history to our servers. The optional merchant advertising platform is a separate service with its own account; see the Privacy Policy.

7. Languages and system requirements

  • Languages: English, Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese.
  • iOS app: iOS 15.0 or later.
  • Web version: a current version of Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox with JavaScript enabled. No installation is required.

8. Web-version troubleshooting

  • Blank or stuck loading screen — hard-refresh, then check that JavaScript is enabled and that a browser extension or content blocker is not blocking nearby-restaurant.pages.dev.
  • “City not found”, or nothing near me — use the map in “Choose your area” and drop a pin at your location; the picker is not limited to any preset city list.
  • Location keeps being denied — check the site permissions in the address bar, allow location for this site, then reload.
  • Banners not appearing — ads are served from our public /banners/active endpoint and need a connection; an empty result simply means no banner is active at that moment.
  • Reporting a web problem — please include the browser name and version, the operating system, and the exact website URL so we can reproduce it.

9. Data sources

Restaurant data is provided by Google and used under the Google Maps Platform Terms. Opening hours and prices can change; please confirm with the restaurant before you go.
